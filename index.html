Privacy & Data Protection

At Pen Tube, we use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data. Your trust is our priority.

1. Data Collection

We only collect the data necessary to provide your Creator Vault. This includes your email for authentication and the prompts/scripts you explicitly choose to save.

2. AI Processing

Generations are processed via secure endpoints to AI providers (like Google Gemini). We do not use your personal vault data to train our own models.

3. Security Architecture

Authentication and Vault storage are secured via Firebase v10 with strict Firestore Security Rules, ensuring users can only read/write their own content.
